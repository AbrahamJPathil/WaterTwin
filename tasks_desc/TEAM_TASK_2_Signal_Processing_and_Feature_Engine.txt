TEAM TASK 2 — SIGNAL PROCESSING & FEATURE ENGINE (v2)

HARDWARE UPDATE: the conductivity probe that was bought is a TDS module
(0–1000 ppm), not a raw EC (µS/cm) probe. Every "EC" field below is renamed
to "TDS". This is mostly a rename — the processing job is identical — with
one addition: readings at or near 1000 ppm are saturated, not necessarily
exactly 1000 ppm, and validation needs to flag that. Temperature compensation is
also now backed by a real sensor (DS18B20), not an assumption.

---

1. Your responsibility

You take raw sensor measurements and convert them into clean, meaningful numerical information that the intelligence system can understand.

You are NOT responsible for deciding:

«"This is fouling."»

You only produce reliable measurements and features.

---

6. Validation

A reading should be marked suspicious when:

outside physical range
impossible jump
missing
unstable
saturated

Example:

Previous pH = 7.2
Current pH = 15.7

This should not silently enter the intelligence system as a normal reading.

TDS-specific: a reading pinned at or above ~990 ppm should be marked
`tds_saturated = true` rather than trusted at face value — the true
concentration could be higher and the module can't tell you by how much.

---

7. Quality score

Generate:

float quality;

between:

0.0 = unusable
1.0 = excellent

The quality score describes the quality of this measurement, NOT whether the sensor itself is healthy.

This distinction is important.

9. Temperature compensation

TDS is strongly affected by temperature, and pH somewhat so. A DS18B20 is
confirmed hardware, so both compensations can use a real, synchronized
temperature reading rather than an assumed constant.

Therefore the processing layer should provide the intelligence layer with
temperature-aware TDS (and pH) information rather than blindly passing raw
values.

Do not invent a compensation formula without checking the actual TDS
module's own documentation — cheap analog TDS boards usually already apply
a fixed-temperature assumption internally, and re-compensating on top of
that can do more harm than good if you don't know what the board itself
assumes.

For the MVP, create an abstraction:

float compensateTDS(
    float rawTDS,
    float temperature
);

The actual implementation can initially be configurable or disabled until
the module's behavior is confirmed empirically (Task 5 can help verify this
with a controlled-temperature experiment).

10. Acoustic processing

Do not immediately assume:

high damping = fouling

That is a research hypothesis.

The project specifically requires controlled experiments comparing clean, artificially fouled and heavily fouled conditions and measuring amplitude, frequency, decay, damping and repeatability.

Your job initially is simply to extract:

amplitude
dominant frequency
decay
SNR

from an acoustic capture. The receive path is a 40 kHz transducer through an
LM358 envelope/ring-down circuit — decay is read as ring-down time (µs)
above a threshold, not a full waveform capture, unless the hardware team
adds one.
